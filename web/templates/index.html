NEURAL ARCHITECTURE

Model Architecture

Structural design combining shared Siamese convolutional encoders, temporal feature difference, multi-head Vision Transformer attention, and U-Net spatial reconstruction.

Model Topology: 767,777 Parameters • 13 Input Bands • 256 Transformer Tokens PyTorch 2.14
COMPONENT 1

Shared Weights Siamese CNN

Applies identical convolutional kernels across both T1 and T2 multi-spectral inputs, projecting 13 raw bands into an aligned feature representation without parameter bloat.

COMPONENT 2

Temporal Feature Difference (|F1 − F2|)

Computes absolute pixel-level difference between aligned feature tensors across three spatial scales, isolating subtle radiance discrepancies caused by urban construction.

COMPONENT 3

Vision Transformer Global Context

Processes 256 tokens (128 dimensions each) through 2-layer multi-head self-attention, modeling macroscopic spatial inter-dependencies across the entire scene without recurrent bottleneck.

COMPONENT 4

U-Net Spatial Reconstruction

Combines transformer global features with high-resolution CNN skip connections across 4 upsampling stages, restoring fine structural edges and building perimeters.

COMPONENT 5

Binary Change Map Output

Applies a final 1×1 convolution with sigmoid activation thresholded at 0.5 to produce a calibrated binary segmentation mask distinguishing changed pixels from stable terrain.

THEORETICAL FOUNDATION

Literature & Research Papers

Foundational 2026 satellite change-detection research papers directly included in the project repository and informing our architectural formulation.

PAPER 01 • 2026

TranSiamUNet based transformer-augmented Siamese-U-Net for precise change detection in satellite imagery

Farid Ali, Soha Safwat Labib, Ayat Mahmoud, Ibrahim Eldesouky Fattoh
+
Journal: Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio, 2026) DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-43164-w File: papers/01.pdf
Key Idea

Proposes TranSiamUNet, augmenting a Siamese U-Net backbone with vision transformer self-attention mechanisms to effectively resolve the semantic gap between localized textural convolutions and macroscopic temporal scene shifts.

Relevance to Our Approach

Directly validates our primary architecture combining shared Siamese convolutional encoders with a 2-layer multi-head Vision Transformer and U-Net decoder with skip connections for bi-temporal Sentinel-2 imagery.

PAPER 02 • 2026

Automated Urban Expansion and Land-Use Change Detection Using Deep Learning Ensembles on Sentinel-2 Imagery

Emadoddin Hemmati, Niloofar Alizadeh, Fatemeh Mahmoudzadeh, Shahin Jafari, Hamed Amini Amirkolaee
+
Publication: ISPRS Annals of Photogrammetry, Remote Sensing and Spatial Information Sciences (2026) DOI: 10.5194/isprs-annals-X-4-W8-2025-349-2026 File: papers/02.pdf
Key Idea

Decomposes semantic land-use transitions into independent binary change-detection tasks evaluated across multi-spectral Sentinel-2 bands using specialized U-Net++ architectural variants.

Relevance to Our Approach

Provides foundational justification for formulating Sentinel-2 urban expansion detection as a binary pixel-level segmentation challenge and benchmarking on multi-temporal spectral constellations.

PAPER 03 • 2026

S2C: A Noise-Resistant Difference Learning Framework for Unsupervised Change Detection in VHR Remote Sensing Images

Lei Ding, Xibing Zuo, Haitao Guo, Jun Lu, Zhihui Gong, Xuanguang Liu, Jicang Lu
+
Institution: Information Engineering University (2026) Year: 2026 File: papers/03.pdf
Key Idea

Introduces a Semantic-to-Change (S2C) framework incorporating noise-resistant contrastive difference learning that translates implicit foundation representations into robust change masks without sensitivity to sensor noise.

Relevance to Our Approach

Directly inspired our noise-robust spatial and spectral augmentation scheme (band scaling, Gaussian perturbation, and independent temporal jitter) which boosted test F1 from 45.93% to 47.44%.

PAPER 04 • 2026

MCD-Mamba: mamba-based framework for infrastructure change detection using multimodal satellite data

Meruyert Kenzhebay, Mehak Khan, Abdul Hanan, Reza Arghandeh
+
Area: Multimodal State-Space Geospatial Analytics (2026) Year: 2026 File: papers/04.pdf
Key Idea

Explores selective state-space sequence modeling (Mamba) as a linear-complexity computational alternative to quadratic transformer self-attention for large-scale multimodal infrastructure change tracking.

Relevance to Our Approach

Serves as a cutting-edge architectural benchmark for long-range sequence modeling, contextualizing our 256-token Vision Transformer attention within modern sequence-modeling paradigms.

PAPER 05 • 2026

A GCN-Mamba-based model for remote sensing change detection using local-global feature aggregation and dual-perspective adaptive fusion

Xueli Chang, Hongyang Dai, Guangqi Xie, Ting Bai, Liqiao Tian
+
Area: Remote Sensing Machine Intelligence (2026) Year: 2026 File: papers/05.pdf
Key Idea

Combines Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN) with state-space models to simultaneously capture irregular spatial topological relationships and long-range temporal change dynamics.

Relevance to Our Approach

Offers theoretical insight into spatial topology modeling versus convolutional inductive bias, reinforcing our design choice of shared convolutional backbones paired with attention mechanisms.
